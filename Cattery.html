Our Cattery is fully insulated, fully roofed, spacious, secure and offers a calm, clean environment to ensure total comfort. Our new large sunrooms are put to good use during the day, allowing your pet time to exercise and play.


At night your pet will sleep in their large cat suite in the fully insulated cattery. You may wish to bring your pet's own favourite toy or blanket to make them feel more at home.


Our dedicated and caring team treat each pet like a very special friend and we can cater for all requirements, to provide the best caring environment during your pet's stay. We offer services as:

  • Single and Large Suites
  • Temperature Controlled Environment
  • Huge Play Area for individual amusement and gym equipment including games on different levels, scratch posts, climbing logs and other items for the amusement of your pet
  • Secure Areas
  • Inside/Outside Access to Secure Sunrooms
  • Day Boarding Available
  • Our Veterinarian is Available at any time, if required
  • F3 Vaccination essential (Original or copy of current Vaccination certificate is required as proof of vaccination)
