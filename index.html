Welcome

Batemans Bay Boarding Kennels takes a professional approach in providing a caring environment for our boarding kennels, with comfortable facilities that allow for the best care while you're away.


Batemans Bay Boarding Kennels is a family-owned and operated kennels located on a beautiful relaxing 15 acre property located 15.8kms South of Batemans Bay (6.1kms South of Mogo and 11kms North of Moruya), at 1554 Princes Highway, Bimbimbie NSW 2536. We offer a safe and tranquil environment for your animals to stay and play.


We have many years of experience, and our team ensures the best pet boarding and pet care services are provided for your animals. The Boarding Kennels are fully insulated, pets are exercised 3 times a day, secure outdoor runs, inside/outside access, special requirements catered for, current C5 (Dogs) and F3 (Cats) vaccination essential and T.L.C is our No 1 priority.


Member of ANIMAL WELFARE LEAGUE (AWL) and Eurobodalla Branch Carer
